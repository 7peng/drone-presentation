The problem

GPS is a single point of failure

Every drone's return-to-launch failsafe assumes it knows where it is. That comes from GPS.

A cheap jammer drowns out satellite signals that are weaker than a light bulb seen from 20,000 km away.

Without a fix, the drone dead-reckons. Small IMU errors compound, and it ends up lost, drifting, or down in the wrong place.

Add one cited stat on jamming frequency, e.g. from a 2025 regulator or ADS-B report.

Our approach

A learned navigator that doesn't need GPS

~45s · 2 clicks. Edit the boxes to match your real architecture.

  • (start) Inputs: only sensors that can't be jammed. IMU, camera, baro and compass, plus the last good GPS fix.
  • (click) An encoder fuses them. A recurrent policy keeps memory of the path flown out, which is what lets it retrace the way home.
  • (click) It outputs velocity commands straight to the flight controller, plus its own confidence. If confidence drops it loiters instead of guessing.
